   M1 ADS126x across a 150 ohm foil shunt (span 0..1.56 V of a 5 V FSR):
      noise 0.10 uV/rtHz -> 0.034 mg/rtHz (negligible after the settling filter)
      INL 3 ppm FSR typ / 12 max = 9.6 / 38 ppm of FS before adjustment vs MET-04 3.85 ppm -> needs a >= 3-point linearity adjustment (CAL-03 masses)
      gain drift 0.5 typ / 4 max ppm/K, LTC6655B 2 ppm/K max, shunt 0.05 ppm/K typ -> electronics span tempco up to 2.5 ppm/K (typ ADC) - compensated against a B4 board TMP117,
      separate from the magnet coefficient; LTC6655 thermal hysteresis (up to 30 ppm after a full -40..85 C cycle) is caught by auto-adjust (MET-07).
   M2 +/-I0 PWM in the coil at    1 kHz: +/-0.42 N square-wave force ->  225.22 nm rigid-body motion at the coil (audible below ~18 kHz); current reversal through 27 mH at 10 V takes 67 us = 7 % of a period
   M2 +/-I0 PWM in the coil at    5 kHz: +/-0.42 N square-wave force ->    9.01 nm rigid-body motion at the coil (audible below ~18 kHz); current reversal through 27 mH at 10 V takes 67 us = 34 % of a period
   M2 +/-I0 PWM in the coil at   20 kHz: +/-0.42 N square-wave force ->    0.56 nm rigid-body motion at the coil (audible below ~18 kHz); current reversal through 27 mH at 10 V takes 67 us = 134 % of a period
      -> rejected: an inaudible 20 kHz version (reversal <= 2 % of a period from 24 V) needs ~21 turns and
         I0 ~350 mA (a power stage and a hot shunt), and the full-scale square-wave force excites the

      G491 timer at 170 MHz, 20 kHz PWM: 118 ppm per period, sigma-delta dithered to << 1 ppm in the loop band (no HRTIM needed);

   The ADS126x is not used inside the loop (sinc latency, noise at high rates); position uses the MCU ADCs.

8. Coil driver, heater, coil temperature, firmware load
   Driver: zero-drift op-amp (OPA2388 class, ISC +/-60 mA) drives the coil directly; I range -2.1..+12.5 mA; coil + shunt return to a ~1.0 V node on a 5 V rail (swing 0.45..4.29 V).
   With 0.5 V of headroom the current slews 19 A/s through 27 mH: a full-scale change takes 0.56 ms (the loop needs ~10 ms). Hardware current limit + COIL_EN gated by SAFE.
   -> OI-04 closes: no power op-amp (OPA593) is needed once Km is 3+.
   Heater: C+D hairpin driven bipolar (3-level) at >= 25 kHz from a small H-bridge on the 3.3-5 V rail, power set
   by duty (P = D V^2/R_h); P_heater = P0 - I^2 R_coil every 10 ms.  Residual hairpin imbalance (~1 % of a turn
   per pair, ASSUMED) gives only an AC force at the drive frequency -> no DC error, inaudible.
   Power accuracy: 2 % of P0 = 0.36 mW -> ~0.40 mg at 520 g (MC §6: 1.1 mg/mW, Recoma 28).
   Coil temperature by resistance (D-060): R_c = R_s x V_coil/V_shunt (ratiometric, ADC2 at 10 SPS), FS (gain 1): V_coil 1182.4 mV -> 6.2 ppm -> 1.6 mK per reading
   Coil temperature by resistance (D-060): R_c = R_s x V_coil/V_shunt (ratiometric, ADC2 at 10 SPS), empty pan (gain 32): V_coil 23.6 mV -> 11.8 ppm -> 3.0 mK per reading
   Firmware: control ISR at 10 kHz (dual ADC -> ratio -> PID/lead/LPF/notch -> sigma-delta -> DAC by DMA,
   hardware-triggered so jitter does not reach the plant) ~400 cycles = 2.4 % of the 170 MHz core; ADS126x DRDY at 1200 SPS; readout filters, heater and thermal model at 10-100 Hz.
